# Tenora Habit Tracker Asset Manifest

Tenora keeps 36 individually addressable habit icons in
`Tenora/Resources/Assets.xcassets/HabitIcons`.

These icons support practical habit identification across the dashboard, editor,
weekly view, detail history, reminders, and widget. They cover health, wellness,
nutrition, faith, learning, home, personal care, connection, and management.

The previous game world, Keeper character, levels, badges, seasonal scenery, and
reward effects are no longer bundled with the app. Habit history is represented by
clear check-ins, completion rates, and weekly patterns instead.
